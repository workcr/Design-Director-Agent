Week 1-2

Tips for Success

  • Consistency: Schedule dedicated time each week. Regular practice builds mastery faster than sporadic work.
  • Quality over Speed: Don't rush. Take time to refine each exercise to professional standards.
  • Self-Critique: Regularly compare your work to SearchSystem™ and other benchmarks. Be honest about gaps.
  • Seek Feedback: Share work with peers, mentors, or design communities. External perspective is invaluable.
  • Document Process: Save iterations and process work. You'll want this for portfolio case studies.
  • Build a Library: Collect inspiration, precedents, and references throughout your journey.
  • Practice Daily: Even 30 minutes of typographic refinement or compositional studies compounds over time.
